The idea behind Pulse

About

You're in bed. Your mind won't stop.

You don't want to open your eyes. You don't want the screen to wake you up. But your thoughts keep racing — worries, plans, everything at once.

Pulse gives you one thing to focus on. A puzzle you solve entirely through vibrations. No screen to look at. No sound needed. Just close your eyes and feel. One thing to hold your attention, so everything else can quiet down.

When to play

For when your mind needs somewhere to land.

Lying in bed. Too many thoughts. Just focus on one thing.
Can't sleep but don't want a bright screen waking you up.
Want to do something, but don't want to open your eyes.
Need a break from everything. Just feel.

Features

Simple by design.

Feel the Numbers
Each number has its own vibration pattern. Swipe to explore, feel to solve.
Voice Mode
Turn on voice and hear the numbers spoken to you. Another way to play without looking.
5 Levels
Start with a simple ring. Work your way up to grids. Easy to learn, satisfying to master.
One Hand, Eyes Closed
Swipe and tap. That's it. No screen needed. Play lying in bed without waking yourself up.

Privacy Policy

Pulse does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services.

Everything stays on your device. Nothing leaves your phone.

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.
